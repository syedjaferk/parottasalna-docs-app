Privacy Policy

{{ brand.PRODUCT }} · Last updated: {{ brand.PRIVACY_UPDATED }}

{{ brand.PRODUCT }} (“we”, “us”) is the online course portal of {{ brand.NAME }}, run by {{ brand.AUTHOR }}. It lets students read course notes, take quizzes and track their progress. This page explains, in plain language, what information the app collects, why, who can see it, and the choices you have.

In short
  • We use Google Sign-In only to confirm who you are: your name, email address and profile picture.
  • We don't access your Gmail, Drive, Contacts or any other Google data, and we don't keep Google access tokens.
  • We store your course progress and quiz scores so you and your instructor can see them.
  • We never sell your data, show ads, or share it for advertising.
  • You can ask us to delete your data at any time.

1. Information we collect

a) From Google Sign-In

You can only sign in with your Google account. When you do, Google shares the following with us, using only the basic profile and email permissions:

  • your name (full, first and last name)
  • your email address, and whether Google has verified it
  • your Google account ID (a number that identifies your account)
  • the web address of your Google profile picture

We do not receive or store your Google password.

b) Enrolment information

Your instructor adds the email addresses of enrolled students to a course. We keep that email address and the course it belongs to, so we know which courses you may open.

c) Your learning activity

When you use a course you are enrolled in, we store:

  • which chapters you mark as complete, and when
  • your quiz answers, scores and the time of each attempt
  • your flashcard progress (which cards you marked as known)
  • the private notes you write on chapters, including any text you quote from the page

Anyone can read our free “common” courses without signing in. For readers who are not enrolled in a course, we store no progress, quiz or notes data for that course.

d) Technical information

  • Cookies that keep you signed in and protect forms (see section 6).
  • Server logs: like most websites, our web server briefly records each request (IP address, date and time, page requested, browser type) to keep the service running and secure.

e) Usage statistics (Google Analytics)

We use Google Analytics to see which pages and courses are read most, so we know what to improve. It records the pages you open, roughly where you are (country/city), your device and browser, and how you arrived (for example from a search). It does not receive your name, email, quiz answers or notes. Google Analytics does not log or store IP addresses, and we have turned off Google signals and ad personalisation, so this data is not used for advertising.

It is not loaded at all if your browser sends a “do not track” signal (Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track). You can also block it with Google's opt-out browser add-on. We do not use tracking pixels or advertising cookies.

3. Google user data

{{ brand.PRODUCT }}'s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • We request only the openid, profile and email scopes, which are needed to sign you in.
  • We use this data only to sign you in and to provide the features described above.
  • We do not keep Google access or refresh tokens, so we cannot act on your Google account after you sign in.
  • We do not transfer Google user data to anyone, except where needed to run the service (our hosting provider), to comply with the law, or with your consent.
  • No person reads your Google data except to provide support you ask for, for security, or to comply with the law.

You can remove {{ brand.PRODUCT }}'s access at any time from your Google Account permissions page.

4. Who can see your data

  • You can see your own progress, quiz results and notes.
  • Your notes are private: no other user, and not your instructor, can see them in the app. Like all data, they are stored in our database and its encrypted backups.
  • Your instructor and the portal administrators can see the names and email addresses of enrolled students, their chapter progress and their quiz answers and scores.
  • Other students cannot see your progress, answers or scores.
  • Our hosting provider stores the data on our behalf on its servers.

We may disclose information if the law requires it.

7. How long we keep data

  • Account, progress, quiz and notes data: for as long as you are enrolled or your account exists, so you can return to your work. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete it within 30 days.
  • Sign-in sessions: they expire after 2 weeks, or when you sign out.
  • Server logs: rotated automatically and kept only for a short time.
  • Backups: kept for 14 days on our server and 30 days (encrypted) on Google Drive; deleted data may remain in a backup until that backup expires.

8. Security

The site is served only over HTTPS. Course pages check your access on every request, quiz results can only be seen by you and your instructor, and we collect only the minimum data needed. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and fix problems quickly.

9. Your rights and choices

You can ask us to:

  • see the personal data we hold about you
  • correct anything that's wrong
  • delete your account, progress, quiz and notes data (you can delete single notes yourself at any time)
  • export your progress and quiz results (your notes can be downloaded from the “My notes” page)

Contact us using the details below. You can also stop using the app and remove its access from your Google Account at any time.

10. Children

{{ brand.PRODUCT }} is intended for learners aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us their data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. If the changes are significant, we will let enrolled students know.
