🧩 Pattern coverage

Aim for about 6 problems per pattern, easy to hard. A ✓ means you recognised the pattern in the placement quiz.

How the cycle works

  1. Solve a new problem and log it with how it went, your confidence and a one-line key insight.
  2. Re-solve it from scratch after 1 day, then 1 week, then 3-4 weeks.
  3. If you can't solve it cold, or needed a hint, it goes back to the start of the queue.
  4. After the third successful re-solve it is mastered and leaves the queue.
